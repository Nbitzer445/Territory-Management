Buying Groups

Branches that buy as one account, combined into the number that actually means something
When a chain raises a PO from one branch and transfers product to another, the revenue lands on a single account while the activity happens somewhere else. Grouping those branches keeps the combined figure as the real one, stops sister-branch comparisons from reading a bookkeeping pattern as a dormant account, and lets whitespace be judged on what the group buys as a whole.
